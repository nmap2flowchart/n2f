NMAP2Flowchart – turn nmap scans into editable network diagrams

Drop in nmap -oX XML and get a zoomable, grid-backed canvas of subnets, hosts, open ports and traceroute hops. Reshape it with layers, sections, notes, icons and hand-drawn connections, then export it to draw.io, Mermaid, or print-ready SVG, PNG and PDF.

  • Any -oX output: ping sweeps, port scans, service and version detection, NSE scripts.
  • Subnets become sections, hosts become cards, and discovery-only scans stay honest – no invented ports.
  • Risk levels colour the diagram and add up in a summary you can hand to a reviewer.
  • Everything runs in your browser: the scan is never uploaded anywhere.

Learn more on GitHub – or press Open sample scan once the editor loads to see a finished diagram.
